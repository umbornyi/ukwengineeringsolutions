Safety First

Uncompromising Compliance & Safety

Operating a lift in Ghana requires strict adherence to national safety standards. At UKW Engineering Solutions, we don't just install elevators; we ensure they are legally compliant and safe for human life.

  • Ghana Standards Authority (GSA)

    We facilitate GSA inspections and ensure your lifts meet the strict requirements of the Weights and Measures Decree.

  • Ghana Building Code (GBC 2018)

    All installations and modernizations are executed in strict accordance with GS-1207:2018 standards.

  • Liability Protection

    Our documented maintenance logs protect property owners and facility managers from legal and insurance liabilities.
